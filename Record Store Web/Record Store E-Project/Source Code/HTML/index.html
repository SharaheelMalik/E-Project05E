Our Story

From a small shop to the city's music landmark

Rhythm House History

Founded in 1985, Rhythm House began as a small record store with just a handful of albums and a dream to share the joy of music with our community.

Over the years, we've grown into the city's premier music destination, known for our extensive collection spanning all genres and formats.

What sets us apart is our commitment to music lovers of all ages - from children's poetry tapes to rare vinyl records for collectors.

Our knowledgeable staff and passion for music continue to make Rhythm House a beloved institution in the city's cultural landscape.
