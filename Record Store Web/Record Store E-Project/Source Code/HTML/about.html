Our Story

From a small shop to the city's music landmark

Rhythm House History

Founded in 1985 by music enthusiast Rajesh Mehta, Rhythm House began as a modest record store with just a handful of albums and a dream to share the joy of music with our community. What started as a small shop in a bustling Mumbai neighborhood has grown into the city's premier music destination.

Our original storefront in 1985

Humble Beginnings

With an initial inventory of just 200 records, Rajesh would personally recommend albums to customers based on their tastes, creating a personalized shopping experience that became our hallmark.

Word quickly spread about the "music guru" who could find any record, and by 1990, we had expanded to carry tapes, then CDs as technology evolved.

Expanding Horizons

In 1995, we moved to our current location on Music Street, allowing us to expand our collection and host in-store performances by local artists.

By 2005, we had become known for our extensive collection spanning all genres and formats, from children's poetry tapes to rare vinyl records for collectors.

Our current location since 1995

Our passionate team of music experts

The Rhythm House Family

What truly sets us apart is our team of passionate music experts who share Rajesh's original vision. Each staff member brings unique expertise - from classical Indian music to underground punk.

Our knowledgeable staff and passion for music continue to make Rhythm House a beloved institution in the city's cultural landscape.

Our Mission

At Rhythm House, we believe music is more than just entertainment - it's a way of life. Our mission is to connect people with the music that moves them, whether they're discovering new sounds or revisiting old favorites.
